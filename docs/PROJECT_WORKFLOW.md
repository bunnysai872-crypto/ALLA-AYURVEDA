# AIIA AYURVEDA – CLINICAL RESEARCH PLATFORM

## Project Workflow

The platform follows this complete clinical research workflow:

Researcher Login
↓
Create Study
↓
Protocol Builder
↓
Document Management
↓
AI Quality Gate

* Completeness Check
* Consistency Check
* Cross-Document Check
* Quality/Risk Flags

↓

Issues Found?

**YES** → Fix & Resubmit → AI Quality Gate

**NO** → Submit to IEC

↓

IEC Secretariat
↓
Document Verification
↓
IEC Review
↓
AI Review Summary
↓
IEC Decision

### IEC Decision

**Approved**
↓
Regulatory & CTRI Tracking
↓
Study Activation

**Modify**
↓
Researcher
↓
Modify & Re-submit
↓
IEC Review

**Not Approved**
↓
Study Not Approved / Record Decision

### Clinical Study Workflow

Study Activation
↓
Participant Management
↓
Informed Consent
↓
Screening & Eligibility Check
↓
Enrolment / Randomization (if applicable)
↓
Clinical Visits
↓
Data Collection
↓
CDISC / FHIR Data Validation

### Pharmacovigilance & Safety

Pharmacovigilance & Safety
↓
Adverse Event (AE)
Serious Adverse Event (SAE)
Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR)
↓
Safety Review & Alerts

### Monitoring & Analytics

Real-Time Study Monitoring
↓
Role-Based KPI Dashboard
↓
AI Risk & Quality Insights
↓
Data Analysis
↓
Final Study Report

### Study Completion

Final Study Report
↓
Study Close-Out
↓
Archive
↓
Audit Trail

## Core User Roles

* Researcher
* IEC Secretariat
* IEC Reviewer
* Administrator

## Important Development Rule

All frontend and backend modules must follow this workflow.

No module should introduce a separate or conflicting workflow without team approval.
